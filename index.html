How to set it for your music
  • The tempo is for the note shown next to the number. In 6/8 it is the dotted quarter (two beats a bar); in 7/8 and other uneven meters it is the eighth note, and the longer beats get louder clicks.
  • If the music says ♩. = 60 in 6/8, choose 6/8 and set 60. If it says ♪ = 160 in 7/8, choose the grouping the music shows and set 160.
  • For music that changes time signature, choose “Your own” and type the bars in order, for example 3/4 3/4 2/4. The eighth note stays the same length across the changes.
  • Set it slow enough that you never stop. Stumbling more than twice? Slow down 10%.
  • Keys: Space start/stop, ← → tempo ±1, T tap.
